ABOUT GPP

Government Polytechnic, Pune

Government Polytechnic, Pune is an autonomous institute of the Government of Maharashtra, established in 1957. It offers diploma programmes in multiple engineering disciplines.

Its vision is to develop self-reliant, versatile and innovative engineers.
