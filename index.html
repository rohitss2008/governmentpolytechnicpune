ABOUT GPP

Government Polytechnic, Pune

Government Polytechnic, Pune is an autonomous institute of the Government of Maharashtra, established in 1957. The institute has a large campus at Ganeshkhind, Pune and offers diploma programmes in multiple engineering and technical disciplines.

Its vision is to develop self-reliant, versatile, innovative and quality-conscious engineers for the betterment of society.
